# MUFS Technology

## The Digital Preservation of Football History

Football history is usually remembered through trophies, statistics and legendary moments.

But behind every era of Manchester United stood millions of supporters who lived every season beside the club.

MUFS exists to symbolically preserve that connection in digital form.

This repository contains the conceptual framework and historical calculations behind MUFS.

---

# Proof of Support

MUFS is built on one central idea:

A football club and its supporters exist as one living connection.

The team creates moments on the pitch.
Supporters give those moments meaning through belief, loyalty and emotion across generations.

Every chant.
Every away game.
Every tear of joy and disappointment.
Every season lived together.

For many supporters, love for a football club becomes part of identity itself.

MUFS preserves that journey as a form of digital football history.

---

# Historical Calculation Framework

The MUFS framework uses official football matches, attendance records and tournament structures to symbolically calculate the historical path of Manchester United across generations.

The conceptual foundation is based on:

- Match attendance
- Match results
- Tournament progression
- Seasonal performance
- Historical continuity

The framework interprets football success not only as trophies —
but as the complete journey lived by both the team and its supporters.

---

# Why Calculations Exist

The calculations are not intended as scientific prediction models or financial guarantees.

They exist as a symbolic historical methodology designed to reflect:

- supporter presence,
- club achievement,
- emotional continuity,
- and the scale of Manchester United history across official competitions.

Every official match contributes to the historical journey.
